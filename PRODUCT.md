# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Beginner French learners using short, playful practice rounds in English or Belarusian. The primary use case is a focused daily session with quick feedback, category progress, review words, and lightweight rewards.

## Product Purpose

French Garden helps a learner build French vocabulary and simple phrases through offline practice modes, daily mixed rounds, and local progress tracking. Success means the learner can start quickly, finish a short session, understand what improved, and know what to review next.

## Brand Personality

Playful, gentle, encouraging. The app should feel like a small private learning garden: warm, friendly, and rewarding without becoming noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense classroom software, generic gamified dashboards, heavy decorative motion, and anything that makes the practice flow feel slow or childish. Motion should clarify progress and completion, not distract from answering.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful practice action obvious.
- Reward consistency with small, meaningful feedback.
- Keep learning states calm, readable, and forgiving.
- Let progress feel tangible without turning the app into a game menu.
- Preserve offline-first simplicity and lightweight performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, large touch targets, clear feedback text, and reduced-motion fallbacks for all motion. The interface should remain usable on small mobile screens and without network access.
